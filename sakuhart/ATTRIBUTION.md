# Attribution — bundled samples

## The demo painting

`samples/demo_target.jpg` is Johannes Vermeer, *Girl with a Pearl Earring*
(c. 1665), which is in the **public domain**; the scan came from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). It is the target
`sakuhart --demo` rebuilds, and it carries no conditions.

## The starter photographs

The 1,000 photographs in `samples/starter/` come from the **Open Images V7**
dataset (Google). Every one is licensed **CC BY 2.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) — the pool was filtered by exact
match on that licence URL, so no ShareAlike, NonCommercial or NoDerivatives
images are present.

CC BY 2.0 asks for credit to the photographer when an image is redistributed.
Each photo below is centre-cropped and resized to 120px; nothing else was changed.

- Photographs: **1000**
- Photographers: **853**
